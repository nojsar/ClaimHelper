# Guide topic queue

Prioritized backlog for the weekly auto-published appeal guide
(`web/appeals/`). The scheduled task takes the TOP topic, writes the guide,
and deletes the line. Add new ideas at whatever priority fits — real search
intent from people who just got a denial letter beats clever keywords.
