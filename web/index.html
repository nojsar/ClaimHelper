Notice of adverse benefit determination

  • Claim A-20418-77, DOS 03/14: adverse benefit determination issued.In plain English: This is the official denial for your March 14 visit.
  • Service not certified. Prior authorization was not obtained per Section 4.2 of the Evidence of Coverage.In plain English: The plan says approval was needed first, and it was not requested.
  • Member may request an internal appeal within 180 calendar days of receipt of this notice.In plain English: You can appeal. You have 180 days from when you got this letter.
  • Supporting documentation, including a letter of medical necessity from the treating provider, may be submitted.In plain English: A letter from your doctor saying why you need it can help.
  • If the determination is upheld, the member may be eligible for external review by an independent review organization.In plain English: If they say no again, an independent reviewer may look at it.

Before you begin.

Will GetMyYes send anything for me?

No. You review every document and decide whether and how to send it. GetMyYes never contacts your insurer or doctor.

What happens to my uploaded documents?

Unsaved guest uploads normally auto-delete after 24 hours. Saved cases follow the Privacy Policy, and you can delete them from Settings.

Is approval guaranteed?

No. Every appeal depends on the plan, facts, evidence, deadline, and reviewer. GetMyYes cannot decide medical necessity or guarantee an outcome.

What if the packet contains an error?

Email support@getmyyes.com within 14 days. Under the accuracy guarantee, it is corrected or refunded when the stated conditions apply.
