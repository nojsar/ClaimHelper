Questions, answered

Can I appeal a denied medication or prior authorization?
Yes. Most health-insurance denials — denied medications, prior authorizations, step-therapy requirements, formulary exclusions, and "not medically necessary" decisions — can be appealed. GetMyYes drafts the appeal paperwork from your denial letter for you to review and send.
What exactly is in the appeal packet?
A plain-English summary of your denial, an appeal letter draft, an evidence checklist, a doctor letter request draft, an insurer call script, a deadline tracker, and a PDF export.
How much does it cost?
The preview — denial type, amount at stake, and likely appeal path — is free. The full appeal packet is a one-time $39 per case. No subscription.
Is this legal or medical advice?
No. GetMyYes is a document drafting assistant. It does not provide medical, legal, or insurance advice, does not decide medical necessity, and does not guarantee approval. You review every document before sending it.
What happens after I send the appeal?
Your packet includes 2 follow-up rounds. When the insurer replies — or ignores you — report what happened and GetMyYes drafts your next move: a second-level appeal, an external-review request, or a status demand letter. Extra rounds are $19 each, or the $59 Full Case upgrade covers up to 100 rounds per case (not unlimited).
Is my health data private?
Your files are processed only to draft your appeal — never to train AI. Uploads auto-delete after 24 hours unless you save your case, and you can delete your case and files at any time.
