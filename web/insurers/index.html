Appeal a denial — by insurer

UPDATED JULY 27, 2026 · U.S. PLANS · NOT LEGAL OR MEDICAL ADVICE

The federal appeal rules are the same almost everywhere — at least 180 days to appeal, independent external review after the final internal denial — but every insurer routes appeals differently: different portals, entities, and quirks. Find yours below.

Insurer-specific appeal guides
InsurerWhat to know
UnitedHealthcareUnitedHealthcare (part of UnitedHealth Group) is the largest health insurer in the U.S., covering employer plans, ACA marketplace plans, Medicare Advantage, and Medicaid plans.
AetnaAetna is part of CVS Health and covers employer plans, ACA marketplace plans in some states, and a large Medicare Advantage book.
CignaCigna Healthcare focuses heavily on employer-sponsored coverage, with pharmacy benefits usually managed by Express Scripts (part of Cigna's Evernorth).
Blue Cross Blue ShieldBlue Cross Blue Shield is not one company — it's an association of dozens of independent, locally operated Blue plans (Horizon, Highmark, Florida Blue, CareFirst, Premera, and many more).
Anthem (Elevance Health)Anthem is the Blue-branded insurer operated by Elevance Health (renamed from Anthem, Inc.
HumanaHumana today is primarily a Medicare Advantage and Medicare drug-plan insurer (it has been exiting employer group coverage).
Kaiser PermanenteKaiser Permanente is an integrated system — the insurer and the medical group are part of the same organization, operating in California and several other states plus D.C.
Ambetter (Centene)Ambetter is Centene's ACA marketplace brand, sold through state-specific companies (Ambetter from …).
Molina HealthcareMolina Healthcare focuses on Medicaid managed care, ACA marketplace plans, and Medicare plans.
Oscar HealthOscar is a technology-focused insurer selling primarily ACA marketplace plans.
