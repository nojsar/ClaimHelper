CO-231: Mutually exclusive procedures

UPDATED JULY 2026 · U.S. PLANS · NOT LEGAL OR MEDICAL ADVICE

Two billed procedures can't be paid together under coding rules. A provider-side coding matter.

CO-231 at a glance
  • Code group: CO — Contractual Obligation — in-network providers generally cannot bill you for CO adjustments.
  • Who usually fixes it: The provider’s billing office.
  • Worth appealing? Rarely — usually fixed by resubmission.

What to do about a CO-231 denial

  1. Ask the billing office to review the code pair; a corrected claim or modifier usually resolves it.
  2. In-network, these write-offs are generally not billable to you.
