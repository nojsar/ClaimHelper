CO-234: Procedure not paid separately

UPDATED JULY 2026 · U.S. PLANS · NOT LEGAL OR MEDICAL ADVICE

The payer treats this procedure as part of another service on the claim. Like CO-97, a bundling rule.

CO-234 at a glance
  • Code group: CO — Contractual Obligation — in-network providers generally cannot bill you for CO adjustments.
  • Who usually fixes it: The provider’s billing office.
  • Worth appealing? Rarely — usually fixed by resubmission.

What to do about a CO-234 denial

  1. The billing office reviews whether a modifier legitimately separates the services and rebills if so.
  2. You generally owe nothing extra for bundled write-offs in-network.
